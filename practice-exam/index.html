Domain 1: Cloud Concepts · 24% of scored content

Answered 0 of 24 · First-try correct: 0

1. A company wants to avoid large upfront investments and pay only for the computing resources it actually consumes. Which cloud benefit does this describe?

Pay-as-you-go pricing trades fixed expense (buying servers) for variable expense — you pay for what you use, with no long-term commitment.

2. What is the core mechanism behind AWS economies of scale?

Because hundreds of thousands of customers aggregate their usage on AWS, everyone achieves economies of scale that translate into lower per-unit prices.

3. A startup wants to reach users in North America, Europe, and Asia without owning infrastructure there. Which AWS capability supports “go global in minutes”?

With a few clicks you can deploy your application into AWS Regions worldwide, putting infrastructure close to users regardless of your company’s own locations.

4. Which statement best captures the benefit of stopping spending money on running and maintaining data centers?

Benefit 1 of cloud computing: stop spending money running and maintaining data centers — invest that effort in customers and the product instead.

5. Why does cloud computing increase speed and agility for a development team?

With resources ready in minutes instead of weeks, teams experiment and iterate faster — the “increased speed and agility” advantage.

6. What is an AWS Region?

A Region is a physical location in the world with multiple Availability Zones; each AZ is isolated but interconnected through low-latency links.

7. What is an Availability Zone?

An AZ is one or more discrete data centers with independent power, cooling, and networking — connected to other AZs in the Region with private, high-bandwidth links.

8. What is the purpose of an edge location?

Edge locations deliver content closer to viewers through CloudFront; they are not Regions, AZs, or general-purpose storage sites.

9. A regulated project must keep certain data physically stored in Germany. Which factor drives the Region choice?

Four factors drive Region selection — latency, price, service availability, and compliance/data residency. Here compliance is decisive (e.g., eu-central-1 Frankfurt).

10. Which of these is NOT a factor when selecting an AWS Region for a workload?

Region selection is about latency, price, service availability, and compliance — the IAM user count is an account setting, not a Regional property.

11. An architect must keep a web tier running even if one data-center building loses power. Which deployment achieves this within one Region?

Multi-AZ deployment isolates failure domains: redundant power/networking is per-AZ, so losing one AZ does not take down the tier.

12. Why can a multi-AZ deployment keep serving traffic when one AZ fails?

Availability Zones are engineered for fault isolation — a failure like a storm in one AZ does not affect others, and private links keep synchronized copies reachable.

13. The AWS Well-Architected Framework has six pillars. Which is the most recently added?

The pillars: Operational Excellence, Security, Reliability, Performance Efficiency, Cost Optimization — and Sustainability (added in 2021) as the sixth.

14. A team reviews spending and deletes idle resources weekly. Which Well-Architected pillar does this practice serve?

Cost Optimization runs the business at the lowest price point — right-sizing, eliminating waste, and matching spend to actual demand.

15. Automating operations, making frequent small reversible changes, and refining procedures after incidents belongs to which pillar?

Operational Excellence covers running and monitoring systems, improving procedures continuously, and keeping changes small and reversible.

16. A workload must automatically recover from component failure and keep meeting demand spikes. Which pillar guides these requirements?

Reliability is the ability to recover from failures and meet demand — recovery procedures, horizontal scaling, and automatic recovery mechanisms.

17. Adopting new serverless technologies to use computing resources more efficiently maps to which pillar?

Performance Efficiency: use computing resources efficiently to meet requirements and adopt the best-fit technologies (serverless, containers) as needs evolve.

18. A CFO compares a three-year server purchase against cloud consumption. Which concept frames the difference?

A core cloud benefit is trading capital expense for variable expense — no upfront hardware; you pay only as you consume.

19. An application’s load doubles every Friday evening and returns to baseline on Monday. Which capability handles this without overprovisioning?

Elasticity means acquiring and releasing resources as demand changes — pay only when the capacity is actually used (e.g., Auto Scaling policies).

20. Before migrating, a finance team wants to compare the total cost of running servers on-premises versus on AWS hour by hour. Which tool fits?

The AWS Pricing Calculator estimates a monthly bill from a workload spec and supports CSV export for TCO conversations on the cloud side.

21. A team uses Amazon RDS instead of self-managing MySQL on EC2. What is the direct benefit?

Managed services remove undifferentiated heavy lifting — with RDS, AWS operates the engine and its OS; the team focuses on data and application logic.

22. How does operating at cloud scale strengthen security posture?

Benefit: increased security posture leveraged at scale — AWS aggregates knowledge of threats across millions of customers into automated protections.

23. A company historically bought servers sized for Black-Friday peak traffic that sat idle 350 days a year. What cloud practice fixes this waste?

Stop guessing capacity: access resources sized to real demand and scale elastically — no more peak-sized hardware sitting idle.

24. Which of the following is a stated advantage of cloud computing over on-premises IT?

The six advantages include trading fixed for variable expense, economies of scale, no capacity guessing, speed and agility, no data-center spend, and going global in minutes.

Domain 2: Security and Compliance · 30% of scored content

Answered 0 of 30 · First-try correct: 0

25. A customer runs a three-tier application on Amazon EC2. Who patches the guest operating system of those instances?

For infrastructure services like EC2, the shared responsibility model assigns guest-OS patching, applications, and data to the customer; AWS secures the hypervisor and facilities.

26. Under the shared responsibility model, who is responsible for patching the database engine of an Amazon RDS instance?

For managed services the split shifts: AWS patches the RDS engine and underlying OS, while customers still own data, network settings, and account configuration.

27. Which duty is ALWAYS a customer responsibility in the cloud, regardless of service type?

AWS never accesses or classifies customer data — data classification, protection choices, and identity management stay with the customer across every service.

28. Which action requires signing in as the account root user?

Root-only tasks include closing the account, changing the account email/contact info, and restoring IAM user permissions — everything else runs on IAM identities.

29. Which controls are AWS MFA device types?

MFA adds a second factor via a hardware device (e.g., a FIDO key) or a virtual TOTP application — recommended on the root account and IAM users.

30. What does the principle of least privilege demand for IAM policies?

Least privilege: start with the minimum permissions and add as needed — never broad wildcards or shared admin credentials.

31. A nightly job needs AWS permissions but must not hold a stored password or access key. What fits best?

Roles provide temporary credentials via STS — ideal for workloads; no long-lived secrets are stored or embedded.

32. Why do teams use IAM groups?

Groups let administrators attach a policy once and manage permissions through membership — clean least-privilege administration.

33. A user’s policy grants s3:GetObject on one bucket while an attached policy explicitly denies it. What happens?

IAM evaluation: an explicit deny in any attached policy overrides all matching allows.

34. By default, what does an IAM user with NO attached policies get?

Every request starts denied; nothing is available until a policy grants it (implicit deny), and an explicit deny overrides allows.

35. What does an account-level password policy control?

The password policy sets account-wide password rules — length, complexity, and rotation — applying to IAM user credentials.

36. A company wants employees signing in once with their corporate directory identities to reach multiple AWS accounts. Which service fits?

IAM Identity Center centralizes workforce identity — federates a corporate directory and assigns permissions across accounts with one sign-in.

37. Which statement about IAM roles is true?

Roles are sets of permissions a principal temporarily assumes via STS — ideal for cross-account access and compute workloads, with no stored credentials.

38. How is data protected while moving between a user’s browser and AWS services?

Encryption in transit uses TLS — every AWS service endpoint speaks HTTPS (SSL/TLS); credentials never traverse the internet unencrypted.

39. An object must remain unreadable if a snapshot of the disk is stolen. Which control addresses this?

Encryption at rest protects stored data: even physical or snapshot access to the medium yields ciphertext without the key.

40. A new object lands in a bucket and nobody sets any encryption option. What happens?

S3 automatically encrypts every new object — server-side encryption with SSE-S3 is the default behavior, at no added cost.

41. What is AWS KMS used for?

AWS Key Management Service manages encryption keys, powers envelope encryption, controls usage with key policies, and logs key use in CloudTrail.

42. A customer-managed symmetric KMS key is left alone after creation. What does KMS do with automatic rotation?

By default, automatic rotation happens every 365 days for customer-managed symmetric keys; the key ID stays the same while key material cycles.

43. An auditor asks who deleted a production S3 bucket and from which IP, two months ago. Which service answers?

CloudTrail records API calls: the action, the identity, the source IP, and the time — the audit log for “who did what”.

44. A developer opens the CloudTrail console and uses Event history without creating a trail. How far back can they look?

Event history in the CloudTrail console shows the last 90 days of management events; trails deliver a durable record beyond that.

45. Which service records HOW a resource was configured and whether the configuration stayed compliant?

Config is the configuration trail: it records resource settings over time and evaluates them against compliance rules (CloudTrail records actions).

46. A security team wants machine-learning detections for compromised credentials and unusual API activity, feeding on CloudTrail, VPC flow logs, and DNS logs. Which service?

GuardDuty is ML threat detection over CloudTrail management events, VPC flow logs, and DNS logs; it produces findings the team can act on.

47. Which service automatically scans EC2 instances, ECR container images, and Lambda functions for software vulnerabilities?

Inspector performs automated vulnerability management — continuous scans of EC2, container images in ECR, and Lambda code for CVEs and exposure.

48. Thousands of S3 buckets may hold customer records; compliance must find where personal identifiable information actually lives. Which service?

Macie applies ML and pattern matching to discover PII and sensitive data across Amazon S3, reporting findings for remediation.

49. A CISO wants one consolidated pane showing findings from GuardDuty, Inspector, and Macie, checked against security standards. Which service?

Security Hub aggregates security findings across AWS services and accounts and evaluates them against standards such as CIS.

50. An audit team needs AWS’s own SOC and PCI compliance documentation WITHOUT opening a support ticket. Which service provides it?

Artifact is the self-service portal for compliance reports and for reviewing and accepting agreements — you download the evidence yourself.

51. A public-facing web app must block common malicious web requests, like injection patterns, at the application layer. Which service?

WAF inspects web requests at layer 7 against rules — including AWS managed rule groups — assembled on a web ACL attached to CloudFront, ALB, or API Gateway.

52. What does AWS Shield Standard provide, and at what cost?

Shield Standard defends against common DDoS layers 3/4 automatically and free; Shield Advanced is the paid tier with 24/7 response for critical workloads.

53. Where do database passwords with REGULARLY scheduled rotation belong?

Secrets Manager stores secrets AND rotates them automatically on a schedule — the natural home for rotating DB credentials; Parameter Store SecureString is the lighter static store.

54. A multinational must satisfy different privacy regimes in Europe and healthcare rules in the United States. What does AWS recommend for compliance planning?

Compliance varies by geography and industry — AWS Compliance resources plus Artifact program agreements let you map obligations region by region.

Domain 3: Cloud Technology and Services · 34% of scored content

Answered 0 of 34 · First-try correct: 0

55. Traffic to a two-instance web tier must be spread across both instances and both AZs. Which pairing performs the distribution?

ELB distributes incoming traffic across registered targets across AZs; Auto Scaling adjusts the target count to demand — the classic paired pattern.

56. An HTTP application needs URL path-based routing between its services. Which load balancer fits?

ALB operates at layer 7 and routes by HTTP request attributes (path, host, headers); NLB works at layer 4 for high-throughput TCP/UDP.

57. A function must run for up to 15 minutes. Which compute option matches this constraint natively?

Lambda runs code without provisioning servers and is capped at 15 minutes per invocation — longer jobs belong on EC2, Fargate, or Step Functions.

58. A team wants to run containers on managed EC2 instance clusters, scheduling tasks itself. Which service fits?

ECS is a container orchestration service with the EC2 launch type (you manage the instance fleet) or the Fargate launch type (serverless — no servers to manage).

59. Which service runs containers WITHOUT requiring customers to provision or manage any servers or clusters?

Fargate is the serverless engine for ECS and EKS — you specify CPU/memory needs and AWS runs the containers; no instance fleet to operate.

60. A company needs a managed Kubernetes control plane while keeping worker nodes under its own management. Which service?

EKS runs certified upstream Kubernetes — AWS operates the control plane across AZs, and the customer manages data-plane nodes (or uses Fargate).

61. A developer just wants to upload code and have capacity provisioning, load balancing, and health monitoring set up around it. Which service?

Beanstalk is a deployment platform: you provide the code and configuration; it provisions the surrounding stack (EC2/ELB/ASG) automatically.

62. A customer needs to run AWS services on its own on-premises data center for low-latency, local data processing. Which option?

Outposts brings AWS-designed hardware and the same APIs into your facility — local compute/storage for latency-sensitive or data-residency-bound work.

63. A compliance dataset is accessed at most once a year; retrieval time of hours is acceptable. Which S3 storage class minimizes cost?

Archive classes trade retrieval speed and per-GB retrieval fees for the lowest storage price — Deep Archive is the cheapest tier for rarely touched data.

64. Objects must AUTOMATICALLY move to a cheaper class after 60 days without access. What sets this up?

Lifecycle rules automate transitions (e.g., move to Infrequent Access, expire versions, abort incomplete uploads) exactly by object age.

65. The durability of S3 Standard storage is commonly quoted as:

S3 is designed for 11 nines of durability by redundantly storing objects across multiple facilities — one of the most-quoted numbers on the exam.

66. A 2 TB data volume must persist and be attachable to a single EC2 instance in one AZ. Which storage type fits?

EBS volumes are network-attached block devices that attach to one instance within one AZ; snapshots copy them into S3 for durability.

67. Which storage is EPHEMERAL — surviving only while the instance runs and lost on stop/termination?

Instance store is physically attached to the host: ideal for temporary data like buffers and caches, wiped when the instance stops or fails.

68. Hundreds of Linux instances in multiple AZs need a shared POSIX file system with elastic capacity. Which service?

EFS provides an elastic NFS file system mountable by thousands of instances across AZs, growing and shrinking with usage.

69. A Windows application cluster needs a managed SMB file share with NTFS semantics. Which service fits?

FSx provides fully managed third-party file systems — Windows/SMB with NTFS, or Lustre for HPC — with native integration into AWS services.

70. A petabyte-scale dataset must be transferred off-site with no internet link and 100Gbps-class throughput. Which service?

The Snow family moves bulk data physically: Snowball Edge units carry up to petabytes per device, bypassing internet transfer entirely.

71. What is a VPC?

A VPC is your own virtual network with chosen CIDR ranges; subnets live in AZs and can be public (route to an internet gateway) or private.

72. What makes a subnet “public”?

Public vs private is purely routing: a subnet whose route table sends 0.0.0.0/0 to an Internet Gateway is public.

73. Instances in a private subnet must download OS updates from the internet WITHOUT being reachable inbound. Which component?

NAT gateway translates private-subnet outbound connections to the internet while blocking inbound initiation — the standard private-egress pattern.

74. Traffic that matches a security group’s inbound allow rule gets a response back. Why is it allowed OUT automatically?

Security groups are stateful: allowed inbound connections automatically receive allowed replies — no separate outbound rule needed.

75. Which statement about Network ACLs is TRUE?

NACLs are stateless, subnet-scoped, evaluate inbound and outbound separately, and apply numbered rules in order — an explicit deny in a lower number wins.

76. A company needs a dedicated private network connection from its data center to AWS with predictable latency — not traversing the public internet. Which service?

Direct Connect is a dedicated physical line into AWS — private, consistent latency, bypassing the internet; easier redundancy comes from VPN backup.

77. The fastest possible connectivity fix is an ENCRYPTED channel over the internet between on-prem and AWS. Which service?

Site-to-Site VPN establishes encrypted IPsec tunnels over the internet in minutes-to-hours; Direct Connect is the slower-to-provision dedicated line.

78. What can Amazon Route 53 do besides resolving DNS names?

Route 53 is AWS DNS — register domains, resolve names, and use health checks/failover to route away from unhealthy endpoints.

79. An enterprise connects hundreds of VPCs and on-prem networks — a full mesh of peerings would be unmanageable. What addresses this?

Transit Gateway is a hub-and-spoke router: attach VPCs and VPN/DX connections once and route centrally instead of maintaining peer-mesh.

80. Two VPCs are peered. A third VPC tries routing traffic THROUGH one of them to reach the other. What happens?

Peering joins exactly two VPCs; traffic does not transit through a peered chain — separate peerings (or Transit Gateway) are needed.

81. A team wants a managed relational database with automated backups, patching, and multi-AZ failover. Which service?

RDS operates relational engines (MySQL, PostgreSQL, MariaDB, Oracle, SQL Server) with automated backup/patching and multi-AZ standby failover.

82. A demanding workload on MySQL needs faster replication and up to 15 read replicas. Which managed database fits?

Aurora is MySQL- and PostgreSQL-compatible with a distributed storage layer — up to 15 Aurora Replicas with fast failover.

83. A game leader board needs single-digit-millisecond reads/writes on a key-value model at internet scale. Which service?

DynamoDB is a serverless NoSQL key-value database delivering single-digit-millisecond performance at any scale.

84. An analyst team runs petabyte-scale OLAP queries for business intelligence. Which service is purpose-built?

Redshift is a petabyte-scale data warehouse using columnar storage and parallel query — the OLAP home for BI workloads (DynamoDB/RDS serve OLTP).

85. Two microservices must communicate asynchronously without waiting on each other. Which pattern AWS supports durably?

SQS queues store messages until consumers process them — decoupling services; Standard is at-least-once, FIFO offers exactly-once ordering.

86. One event must fan out to MANY subscribers — apps, email, and mobile push — simultaneously. Which service?

SNS is pub/sub push messaging: one publisher, many subscribers (SQS queues, Lambda, HTTP, email, SMS, push).

87. A workflow MUST run every weekday at 08:00 and route change events to different targets by pattern. Which service handles both?

EventBridge routes events between services by rule (event patterns) and delivers scheduled runs at cron-style times — the SNS/SQS-native scheduler for timers.

88. Multi-step processing with retries, choices, and parallel branches must be coordinated reliably. Which service orchestrates it?

Step Functions coordinates workflows as state machines with task retries, branching, and parallel execution — visible state and error handling built in.

Domain 4: Billing, Pricing, and Support · 12% of scored content

Answered 0 of 12 · First-try correct: 0

89. Which pricing principle describes paying only for what you use, with no long-term contract?

The core AWS pricing principle: pay-as-you-go — pay only when consuming, stop paying when done, no upfront lock-in.

90. A fault-tolerant batch job tolerates interruptions and should cost as little as possible. Which purchasing option?

Spot uses spare EC2 capacity at up to 90% off, reclaimable with a two-minute warning — the natural fit for interruptible, fault-tolerant work.

91. Steady-state production has run the same instance type for months. Which option cuts the bill the most for a 3-year commitment?

Reserved Instances offer the deepest discount (up to 72%) in exchange for 1- or 3-year commitments, with all/partial/no upfront payment windows.

92. A team’s usage spans many instance types and OSes, changing monthly. What does a Savings Plans commitment actually buy?

Savings Plans commit to a consistent dollar amount per hour for 1 or 3 years — the discount follows eligible usage across instance families/OSes.

93. Inbound data transfer across services is generally:

The rulebook: data IN is free; data OUT is billed (~10 cents/GB class rates, with the first 100 GB/month free on internet egress per current docs).

94. A team must forecast what a new architecture will cost per month BEFORE migrating. Which tool?

The Pricing Calculator models services and regions into a monthly estimate (exportable CSV) for planning and TCO comparisons.

95. What are the three default alert thresholds the My Monthly Cost Budget template creates on a $10 budget?

The template wires alerts at actual 85%, actual 100%, and forecast 100% — email recipients must be verified before alerts fire.

96. Which tool charts 13 months of usage history and forecasts spending 12 months ahead, filterable by service/tag?

Cost Explorer visualizes and forecasts spend — 13 months of history, up to 12 months forward, sliced by service, tag, region, and more.

97. Finance needs THE most detailed record of AWS usage, loadable into Athena for querying. Which deliverable?

CUR delivers the most granular usage/cost data as zipped files (split up to 500 MB) to S3 daily — query it with Amazon Athena.

98. Under consolidated billing in AWS Organizations, how do volume discounts and Reserved Instances behave across member accounts?

Consolidated billing puts all accounts on one payer bill: usage consolidates for pooled tiering, and RI/Savings Plan benefits are shared across members.

99. A large account needs designated technical account management and 15-minute response support. What does the current Enterprise Support plan require?

Current docs: Enterprise Support carries a designated TAM, 15-minute response SLAs, and a minimum cut from $15,000 to $5,000/month (legacy tiers end Jan 1, 2027).

100. Cost Explorer cannot group spending by a project tag. What was skipped?

Cost allocation tags must be ACTIVATED in the Billing console before Cost Explorer or the CUR can group with them; activation applies from then onward.
